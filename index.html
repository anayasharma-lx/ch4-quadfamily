Every quadrilateral belongs to a family. Children inherit all properties of their parents and gain new ones. Click any region to explore.

Reading the diagram: regions nested inside other regions are special cases. A square sits inside both Rectangle and Rhombus — it has every property of both. Kite and Trapezium are separate branches that don't inherit from each other.
